PF & ESIC Statutory Rates

PF is governed by the EPF & MP Act 1952 and ESI by the ESI Act 1948, both central statutes, so Gujarat uses the notified national rates. PF applies to Basic only, capped at the wage ceiling and pro-rated for loss of pay. ESI applies to the whole earned wage; overtime counts towards the contribution but does not decide coverage. Both are switched on per employee in Employee Master and apply to monthly wage employees only.

Daily Wage Attendance Bonus

Daily wage employees are not offered leave. In its place they earn a monthly attendance bonus. Absence is measured against a full working day and includes late arrival and early leaving, so a day paid in full can still carry absence minutes. Week offs and holidays never count as absence.
