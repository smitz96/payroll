Each day is judged on working hours against its shift. At or above the shift length less the check-in and check-out grace, nothing is charged; below that, the shortfall against the full shift is charged as less hours, rounded up to 15 minutes. Overtime is paid once hours reach the shift length plus its OT grace, on the time beyond the shift, rounded down to 15 minutes. Check-in and check-out grace are 0 unless set; OT grace starts at 30. A full day needs 2/3 of the shift and a half day 1/3. Weekdays without a shift of their own work the default shift. Changes apply to open months when they are recalculated.
